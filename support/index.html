Common questions

A site I trust was flagged. What do I do?

Warnings are based on warning signs, so they can sometimes be wrong. On the warning page you can continue to the site. In the popup, choose "Always trust this site" for a site you know is fine.

How do I report a phishing site?

Open the Septimius popup on the page and press "Report Phishing". A real person reviews every report.

How do I switch a feature off?

Open Septimius Options. You can change how sensitive it is, switch notifications and the full-page warning on or off, and switch each optional extra tool on or off.

Safe Preview is slow the first time. Is something wrong?

No. The first use after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up. After that it is quick.

What does Septimius send, and where?

Please read the privacy policy. It lists everything that stays on your device and everything that is sent.

Is Septimius an antivirus?

No. It warns you about phishing websites and scams. It is not a replacement for antivirus software or for careful judgement.
