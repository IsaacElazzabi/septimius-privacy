Septimius – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Septimius is a free extension for Microsoft Edge that warns you about phishing websites. It is made by Isaac Elazzabi, an independent developer. Questions or requests: septimius.support@gmail.com. Septimius is not made by, or affiliated with, Microsoft.

1. The short version

  • Checks of the pages you visit run on your device. Septimius does not collect your browsing history and does not read or send the text of the pages you visit.
  • Information leaves your device only in the situations listed in section 3.
  • There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

2. What stays on your device

Your settings, a copy of the list of known dangerous sites, your recent warnings, the sites you chose to trust, and (only if you take part in the community features) a random ID that Septimius creates for itself. That ID is not linked to your name, e-mail address or accounts. Uninstalling Septimius removes this data from your device.

3. What is sent, and when

  • Threat list download (automatic, about twice a day). Septimius downloads the public list of known dangerous sites from the Septimius server. The request contains nothing about you or the sites you visit.
  • When the on-device list is not available. For example right after installing, or if the list could not be refreshed for over a week. Septimius then asks the server whether the site you are opening is a known threat. Only the site's name (for example “example.com”) is sent, not the full address, the page content or who you are. The server uses it only to answer.
  • When you press a button. “Check Domain Age” sends the site's name; “Check Database” sends the address you typed or the current site; “Check VirusTotal” sends the address to the VirusTotal service; “Check IP” sends the IP address to the AbuseIPDB service. Each goes through the Septimius server.
  • An optional setting, off by default. “Look up domain age automatically” sends the name (only the name) of each new site you open, to find its age. You can switch it off at any time in Options.
  • Community features (optional). When you report a site, Septimius sends the site's address, your optional note and your random ID. If you choose to be credited, your chosen display name is sent too. If you share a comment, its text is sent. A person reviews every report, name and comment. Nothing is shown publicly or added to the threat list until it is approved, and a name appears only after it is approved.

4. Who receives data

The Septimius server and its database run on hosting services (Render and Supabase) that process data on our behalf. For the checks you start yourself, the server contacts VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB and domain-registration (RDAP/WHOIS) services. We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising, analytics or any purpose unrelated to the extension.

5. What the server stores

Only for people who use the community features: a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your random ID (never the ID itself), your optional display name and the date you agreed to be credited, your reports (address, optional note, date) and your comments. The server does not store e-mail addresses or IP addresses. Connection details such as an IP address are used briefly to limit abuse, and the hosting provider may handle standard technical connection data in order to run the service. Approved reports add only a site address to the public threat list, with no personal details.

6. Your choices and your information

  • You can use Septimius without any optional feature, and nothing personal leaves your device.
  • You can turn off automatic domain-age lookups in Options at any time.
  • “Forget me” in the community panel deletes your profile (the ID hash and your name) immediately. Reports you already sent stay as records but no longer point to you.
  • To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, e-mail us and we will deal with it.

7. Contributions

A welcome page offers an optional one-time contribution. It is a link that opens PayPal's website in a new tab. Septimius never sees or stores your payment details, and PayPal's own privacy policy applies there. Contributing is never required and does not change what Septimius does. It is a voluntary contribution to support development, not a charitable donation.

8. Security

All communication between Septimius and its server uses HTTPS.

9. Children

Septimius is not directed at children under 13. and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example a name in the community features), e-mail septimius.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page.
