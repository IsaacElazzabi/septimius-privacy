SEPTIMIUS

Phishing protection that warns you before you type a password

Septimius is a free browser extension. It checks every page you open for the signs of phishing, explains what it found in plain words, and puts a full-page warning in front of known threats.

Add to Firefox Add to Edge Chrome: coming soon
  • Full-page warning for known threats, so you can go back to safety before you type anything.
  • A risk score with the reasons, in plain English, for any page you open.
  • Look-alike detection for fake addresses such as a number 1 in place of the letter l.
  • Optional extra tools: a fake-alert guard and Safe Preview, which shows where a link really goes without opening it.
